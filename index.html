Field Notes
Where AI work fails.
Six patterns we see over and over — and the move we make instead.
Pattern 01

A strategy deck is not a system.

Agencies ship research, wireframes, and a handoff to "your team." Nothing runs in production.

Our moveThe deliverable is running software, not a slide deck. We stay on it until it ships and stabilizes.
Pattern 02

Most AI work leaves you renting.

Platforms and frameworks that lock you in. You pay forever to access workflows you helped build.

Our moveWe hand you the code and the infra. It runs on your servers, under your accounts, with your keys.
Pattern 03

Nobody audits before building.

Everyone jumps to the solution. Nobody asks whether the foundation can support it. Broken processes get automated.

Our moveWe compress discovery into a single week. Understand the problem before we write a line of code.
Pattern 04

If the metric isn't yours, it doesn't count.

Dashboards full of activity metrics. None tell you whether the work paid off.

Our moveWe tie every build to numbers you care about: hours back, dollars in, accuracy up, cycle time down.
Pattern 05

A chatbot in a trench coat is not an agent.

Prompt wrappers sold as AI agents. No tools, no memory, no integration with your systems. Just a text box.

Our moveOur agents use tools, access your systems, make decisions, and execute multi-step workflows end to end.
Pattern 06

Long discovery is a red flag.

Six-week discovery phases that bill time without shipping anything. You're paying for someone to learn your business.

Our moveDiscovery is one week. Then we build. You'll have working software before most firms finish their audit.

Autonomous Report Agent

01 — Overview

A growing professional services firm was spending 15+ hours per week compiling executive reports manually. Data lived in 6 different systems — Snowflake, Google Sheets, Salesforce, QuickBooks, HubSpot, and internal spreadsheets. Every Friday, an analyst would pull data from each source, paste into a template, write commentary, and email it to the leadership team.

We built an autonomous agent that does the entire workflow — data collection, transformation, insight generation, and delivery — without human intervention.

Client
Professional Services Firm
Industry: Professional Services
Use case: Automated reporting
Engagement: Build + Retainer
Timeline: 6 weeks

02 — Challenge

The reporting process was painful for three reasons. First, data was scattered across 6 systems with no unified warehouse — every report started with an hour of data gathering. Second, the analyst had to interpret trends and write narrative commentary, which took domain expertise and couldn't be delegated. Third, reports were always late because the process took so long that any delay in one step cascaded.

The firm had tried off-the-shelf BI tools but they only solved the visualization layer — someone still had to write the executive summary and deliver it in the right format.

03 — What we built

01/05

Data warehouse with automated ingestion

Snowflake warehouse with Fivetran connectors pulling from all 6 data sources on a daily schedule. dbt models transform raw data into analysis-ready tables.

02/05

28 dbt models with 138 automated tests

Staging models, intermediate transforms, and final mart tables — each with schema tests, accepted value tests, and custom data quality checks.

03/05

AI report generation agent

A Claude-powered agent that queries the warehouse, identifies trends and anomalies, generates written executive commentary, and formats everything into a polished report.

04/05

Automated delivery pipeline

Reports generated every Friday at 6am and delivered to Slack and email. Stakeholders can also interact with the agent via Streamlit for ad-hoc questions.

05/05

Interactive Q&A dashboard

Streamlit application where executives can ask natural language questions about the data and get instant answers with supporting charts.

04 — Outcome

0

Human intervention required

The analyst who previously spent 15 hours per week on reporting now spends that time on strategic analysis. Reports are delivered on time every week, in a consistent format, with AI-generated insights that surface trends humans were missing.

05 — Takeaways

Data foundation first

We spent the first 3 weeks on the warehouse and dbt models before touching the AI layer. Without clean, tested data, the agent would have generated unreliable reports.

AI augments, not replaces

The agent handles the repetitive compilation and first-pass analysis. The leadership team still makes the decisions — they just have better information, faster.

Methodology
Four pillars. One framework.
We evaluate your operation through four lenses to find where AI creates the most value.

Repetitive Tasks

Work that follows the same pattern every time — and drains your team's capacity for higher-value thinking.

  • Data entry and transfer between systems
  • Report compilation and formatting
  • Email triage and response drafting
  • Invoice processing and matching

Time-Consuming Processes

Workflows that eat hours every week because they're manual, multi-step, or require waiting on people.

  • Client onboarding and intake
  • Research and competitive analysis
  • Approval routing and follow-ups
  • Compliance documentation

Scalable Opportunities

Processes that would deliver more value if they could handle 10x the volume without 10x the headcount.

  • Sales outreach and lead qualification
  • Content generation and personalization
  • Customer support and ticket resolution
  • Data analysis and insight generation

Error-Prone Activities

Work where human mistakes create costly rework, compliance risk, or customer impact.

  • Financial reconciliation and matching
  • Data validation and quality checks
  • Contract review and extraction
  • Regulatory filing and reporting
